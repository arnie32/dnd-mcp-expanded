Yes. In fact, before writing mechanics, I would build the class like a **5e subclass progression chart** so you can see the whole experience from level 1-20.

The biggest design decision is:

### Do you want subclasses at Level 1 or Level 3?

For Fatebroker, I strongly recommend **Level 1**.

Reason:

An Arbiter, Patronus, and Umbra Dominus should feel different from session one.

A rapier duelist, pistol officer, and shadow assassin shouldn't play the same for three levels.

# Follower Progression

This is the mechanic that really differentiates the Houses.

| Level | Arbiter         | Patronus   | Umbra Dominus |
| ----- | --------------- | ---------- | ------------- |
| 3     | 1 Redeemed Ally | 1 Retainer | 1 Shadow      |
| 6     | 2               | 2          | 2             |
| 10    | 3               | 4          | 4             |
| 14    | 4               | 6          | 6             |
| 18    | 5               | 8          | 10            |

This creates three completely different fantasies:

* **Arbiter** = small band of devoted companions
* **Patronus** = growing mercenary organization
* **Umbra Dominus** = expanding shadow legion

while every Fatebroker still feels like a teleporting time-manipulating recruiter descended from the fallen Imperium Aeternus.

# Signature Capstone Fantasy

| House             | Level 20 Fantasy                                                                                 |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Arbiter**       | Redeem legendary villains and alter the course of destiny itself.                                |
| **Patronus**      | Acquire kingdoms, guilds, mercenary companies, and organizations through leverage and influence. |
| **Umbra Dominus** | Rule a shadow kingdom of fallen futures and command a legion of extracted shadows.               |

This layout gives the class a very clean progression:

* **Core Fatebroker** = Time Magic + Teleportation + Recruitment
* **Arbiter** = Heroic Temporal Duelist
* **Patronus** = Imperial Gunslinger & Contract Lord
* **Umbra Dominus** = Shadow Assassin Monarch

All three share the same Fatebroker chassis while feeling radically different by level 5 and completely distinct by level 10.
